# Waypoint Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**. I usually reply within a few days.

To help me fix a bug fast, please include:
- Your Waypoint version and build. They're at the bottom of **Profile**, for example "v1.0.0 (142)".
- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

## Common questions

**The map doesn't show where I am.**
Go to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Waypoint** and choose **While Using the App**. Turn on **Precise Location** for accurate directions.

**Directions stop updating when I switch apps.**
For background guidance during navigation, allow **Always** location access. Waypoint only uses it while a trip is active.

**How do I delete my account?**
Open **Profile** and tap **Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your account, your synced backup, and your traffic reports. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**A speed limit, speed camera, or place is wrong.**
Speed limits and camera locations come from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org), which anyone can correct, and places come from Apple and Google. Email me the details and I'll pass them on or fix them where I can.

**Is Waypoint available outside the US?**
Not yet.
